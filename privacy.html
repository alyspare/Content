Privacy Policy

Blyssful Travels Content Studio · Last updated November 27, 2026

The Blyssful Travels Content Studio is a private content planner used only by its owner to plan posts and review the performance of the Blyssful Travels social media accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it does not connect to anyone else's account.

What information the app accesses

When the owner connects their own Instagram professional account, the app reads:

  • Basic account details (username and account ID).
  • The owner's own stories and posts, such as the post date, link, caption and preview image.
  • Insights for those stories and posts, such as views, reach, replies, shares, profile visits and follows.

The app does not read direct messages, does not publish or change anything on Instagram, and does not access other people's accounts or personal information.

How the information is used

The information is used only to fill in the owner's content planner, for example to log story results and compare how posts performed. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising or given to any third party.

Where the information is stored

  • Data is stored in the owner's own browser and in a private, encrypted backup in the owner's own GitHub account, so the planner can sync between the owner's devices.
  • The Instagram access token is stored encrypted and is used only to request the owner's own data from Instagram.
  • No other server or database receives this information.

Data deletion

To remove access and delete data:

  • Disconnect the app in Instagram under Settings and privacy → Website permissions → Apps and websites, or use "Disconnect Instagram" in the planner. Disconnecting deletes the stored access token.
  • Imported story and post data can be deleted at any time from the planner, which also removes it from the encrypted backup.

Because the app is used only by the account owner, no data about any other person is collected or kept.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the Blyssful Travels account owner by direct message on Instagram.
