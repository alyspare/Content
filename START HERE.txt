BLYSSFUL CONTENT PLANNER - START HERE
=====================================

This is your Content Planner as your own web page, hosted in your GitHub
account like your Personal Planner. It saves on each device and syncs,
locked with your passphrase, through the same GitHub key and passphrase
your Personal Planner already uses.

PUT IT ON GITHUB (one time, on a computer)
  1. On github.com: + (top right) > New repository. Name it Content,
     set it to Public, and don't tick "Add a README". Create repository.
  2. Click "uploading an existing file" and drag in everything inside
     this Content folder (not the folder itself). Commit changes.
  3. Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > / (root) > Save.
  4. After a minute it's live at https://alyspare.github.io/Content/

TURN IT ON
  Open https://alyspare.github.io/Content/ in the same browser where
  your Personal Planner syncs. It picks up the Planner's sync by itself.
  On iPhone and iPad: open it in Safari, Share > Add to Home Screen.
  If the button at the top says "Sync off", tap it and enter the same
  GitHub key and passphrase you use in the Personal Planner.

BRING YOUR POSTS OVER FROM CLAUDE (one time, on one device)
  Tap the sync button at the top > Import file > pick
  content-planner-move-from-claude.json. Your posts, follower counts,
  tab order and preview photos come across and sync to your other devices.

PERSONAL PLANNER
  Your posts now show in the Personal Planner automatically. There's no
  more Copy and Paste.

UPDATES
  When you get new files, open the repository > Add file > Upload files,
  drag them in and commit. Your entries aren't affected.
